Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-05 · Developer: Riverwon2

Your focus history stays on your Mac. Moro does not send it to a developer-operated server.

Information stored by Moro

Moro stores timer settings, color preferences, appearance and rest-end pulse settings, history visibility, and daily completed focus-cycle counts and focus minutes in your Mac’s app preferences. These values provide the timer, history and customization features. Records are attributed to the date a focus session completes.

The current app has no developer-operated account system, advertising, analytics SDK, cloud sync or developer-operated backend. It does not upload your focus history or settings to the developer.

Purchases through Apple

Custom Colors is an optional non-consumable in-app purchase. Apple processes payment and supplies StoreKit product and transaction information, including verified purchase entitlement and revocation status, so Moro can enable or restore the feature. Moro does not receive your card details or Apple Account password. Apple’s handling of purchase data is covered by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Purchase restoration restores access to Custom Colors. It does not restore local history or saved colors from another Mac.

Streak images and the clipboard

When you choose “Capture my streak,” Moro renders its own streak grid and writes the image to the system clipboard. It does not capture your desktop. The image may be accessible to apps you paste into and to system features such as Universal Clipboard when enabled. Moro does not upload it.

Support messages

If you email the developer, the developer receives your email address and the content and attachments you choose to send. These are used to answer your request and resolve the issue, through the developer’s email provider. Do not send passwords, payment card details or sensitive information.

Support correspondence is kept while needed to handle your request and follow-up, or to meet applicable legal obligations. You may request deletion of support correspondence by emailing the contact below; information legally required to be retained may be kept.

Retention and deletion

Focus history and preferences stay locally until the app’s local data is removed. The 30-day grid is a display window, not an automatic deletion schedule. Reset resets the current timer; Restore default colors resets colors. Neither action deletes focus history. Removing the app alone may leave preferences behind.

The developer cannot remotely access or erase data on your Mac. Contact support for instructions to remove Moro’s local preferences and history. Purchase records held by Apple are managed by Apple; deleting local data does not cancel or refund a purchase.

You can clear a captured image by copying something else. Images you have already pasted or saved must be removed from those destinations separately.

This website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. The developer has added no analytics scripts, advertising, tracking pixels, forms or cookies. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement for its data practices. Opening links to Apple or other services is subject to their policies.
