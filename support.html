Quick start

  1. Open Moro, then click the timer in your Mac’s menu bar. Moro does not open a main window at launch.
  2. Adjust the Focus, Rest and Cycles dials, then select Start.
  3. Select the gear icon for colors, appearance and rest-end pulse settings.
  4. Use × to close the panel. Use … → Quit Moro to quit the app.

Common questions

I can’t find Moro after opening it.

Look for the time in the menu bar. On Macs with many menu-bar items or a display notch, some items may be obscured. Closing another menu-bar utility or using a display with more available space may help.

How do I change colors?

Open Settings → Custom Colors. The optional one-time purchase unlocks unlimited Focus and Rest color changes. The purchase confirmation shows your local price. There is no subscription. Default colors and the core timer remain free.

How do I restore a purchase?

Use the Apple Account used for the original App Store purchase, open Moro’s Settings and select Restore Purchases. An internet connection and Apple authentication may be needed. Restoration restores the color feature, not local history or saved colors.

If no purchase is found, check the account used for the original purchase and contact support if the problem continues.

What happens while a purchase awaits approval?

Custom Colors remains locked until Apple approves the purchase. Continue using the free timer. After approval, reopen Moro and use Restore Purchases if access has not appeared.

How do I request a refund?

Apple handles App Store refund requests and determines eligibility. Follow Apple’s refund instructions. After a verified refund or revocation, Moro uses default colors and locks color editing. Your local focus history and stored color preferences are retained.

What does the streak record?

Each completed Focus session counts as one completed cycle. The last 30 days are shown in the grid; hover over a cell to see that date’s recorded focus time and cycle count. The final Focus session ends the configured sequence without an additional Rest phase.

Can I copy my streak?

Select … → Capture my streak. Moro copies just the streak grid as an image; paste it into an app that accepts images.

How do I remove local history and settings?

Reset affects the current timer and does not erase history. Removing the app may leave preferences on your Mac. Email support for instructions to remove Moro’s local data. The developer cannot delete local data remotely. See the Privacy Policy for retention details.
